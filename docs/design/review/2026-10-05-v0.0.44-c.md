## Bottom line

The wave-2 mechanisms are real and the strongest pins (W3/W4/W11/W13) genuinely discriminate. But the wave violated its own W17 test-hygiene standard twice (C4, C5), placed four pin blocks where they can never redden their suites (C1 — the exact failure mode the claims-need-tests rule exists for), and the W2 closure claim is falsified by the finalize read (C2) plus the untrusted seed (C3). Converging: wave 3 is placement + two small source moves + one default narrowing.
