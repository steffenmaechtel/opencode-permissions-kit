## Test-coverage scorecard (the wave's own hard rule)

13 of 27 implemented claims have no pin at all; 2 pins are non-discriminating (V8, V9); 7 behavioral + 6 static + 1 mixed discriminate. No resolution row *names* a pin it doesn't have — the honesty gap is omission (W13–W16), concentrated in exactly the never/aborts-shaped claims the same-day concept rule (commit `1ee4076`) says owe a behavioral test. The wave violated its own rule it added hours earlier.

## Bottom line

All 28 fixes exist and do what their commits claim at the mechanism level — no disposition is fabricated, no suite broke, the full gate is green. But the wave review earns its keep: the V3 closure is falsified at class level by a renameable stage (W1, HIGH, zero-race root write-through — the strongest finding of the whole 0.0.44 cycle), V2/V5 each missed a sibling form (W3 `x/..`, W4 2→1 downgrade), and the claims-vs-tests audit shows the hard rule was written and then not followed (10 unpinned never/aborts claims). Fix wave 2: W1–W4 first, W5–W10 ride-along, W11–W17 make the pins discriminate.
