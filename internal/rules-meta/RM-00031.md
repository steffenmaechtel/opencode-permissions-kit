A new rationale is unproven: it has never survived a revisit, so it
gets the fastest check first. While the system itself is young this
also exercises the revisit workflow early, when the corpus is small
and a monthly run is cheap. Hand-picked initial intervals (e.g. 2y
for "stable-looking" rules) were considered and superseded — the
ladder derives volatility from evidence, not from first impressions.

Check: new metas enter with `revisit-interval: 1m` regardless of
their topic's perceived volatility.
