0.0.39l showed ceiling pressure even on a converged tree (install.sh
mid-sections "spot checks only", tui assets deferred), and there is
no all-Flash snapshot-scope-pass dataset on a rotted tree — the deep passes
0.0.38/e/g ran on the strong model. Minutes alone would silently
trade coverage for clock: agents cannot watch clocks, they spend
sections — so the coverage map (every shipped file gets at least one
grep-anchored look, every deferral recorded) is the real budget and
the minutes only its ceiling. Same caveat as the diff-scope budget
rule: the minutes are guidance, not enforced limits — subagents
frequently run longer; checkpoint timestamps are the running
evidence.

Check: high-tier passes carry a coverage map; deferrals appear in
the checkpoint as deferrals, not as absence.
