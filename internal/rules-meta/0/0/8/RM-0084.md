Verified in the pinned checkouts: opencode 1.x (v1.18.34) registers
`/review` as `subtask: true` — the template runs inside ONE subagent
that cannot launch subagents of its own — while 2.x (v2.0.22)
injects it into the main session where the lead can fan out. Same
model, prompt and tree class: the 1.x shape produced 2 LOW + 2 INFO
in ~8 min (0.0.45b), the 2.x shape 2 HIGH + 6 MED + 25 LOW/INFO in
~55 min (0.0.45c). The corrected calibration record adds: every deep
full-scope yield so far came from a fan-out-capable (external v2-
shape) pass — full scope without fan-out is structurally
underpowered, wave/diff scope is not. Maintainer decision
2026-10-19: the fan-out-capable shape is preferred for diff/wave
scope too — sequential single-context passes trade breadth even at
diff scope; the subtask form is a fallback, not a peer choice.

Check: pinned-checkout behavior of the installed opencode majors
still matches (subtask vs main-session dispatch); full-scope passes
