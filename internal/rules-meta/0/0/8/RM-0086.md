End-synthesis turns are the primary failure mode of reviewer
subagents: the per-section findings live only in context, and a
context that crashes, truncates or summarizes loses them silently —
observed repeatedly across the 0.0.39 loop's parallel passes. The
observed losses concentrated on ONE provider (opencode-go): tokens/s
so slow that a thinking subagent hit its ~10-minute timeout, and the
main agent received only a "Failed" status — with no hint that the
subagent had already done ~38 minutes of work. A side file appended
after every completed section survives the agent; the `clean` line
for empty sections is what makes an absent entry distinguishable
from a lost one.

Check: checkpoint side files carry one line per completed section
(incl. `clean`), and snapshots assemble from checkpoints rather than
final-context recollection.

Source: internal/records/review-calibration.md, "Budget evolution"
(checkpoint practice from the 0.0.39 loop).
