objective breakage, and this repo's pattern for invariants is
fail-loud CI pins (test-sandbox-policy, test-workflows). Overdue
revisits are a signal about content age, not correctness: blocking on
them would train maintainers to bump dates without reviewing, and
date-inflation defeats the system silently.
