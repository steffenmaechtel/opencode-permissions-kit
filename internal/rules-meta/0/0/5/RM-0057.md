The bound is empirical, not derived: the 0.0.39d wave diff (~2.5k
lines) is the largest diff-scope pass in the corpus, and it marked
the ceiling of what one review pass digests — smaller diffs were
reviewed with materially better coverage. The number exists so round
planning splits before reviewing, not after.

Check: the next round diff approaching ~2.5k lines either gets split
or its snapshot records the breadth loss.
