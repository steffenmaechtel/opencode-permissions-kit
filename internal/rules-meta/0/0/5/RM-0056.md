Rule files are hot-path reading: AGENTS.md routes every agent
session through them, so every line of evidence detail there costs
every session. Metas are the revisit path — read when a rule's
rationale is questioned, exactly where the incidents, suite
sections, dates and caveats belong. Decision 4 already bans dates
and history inline; this extends the same discipline to evidence
detail: the rule unit carries the rule plus a one-line why, the
rationale carries the chain. First applied 2026-10-18: the DD15
mutation-check block moved out of the real-binary-tier rule and the
issue-116 version-floor story moved out of the environment-matrix
rule, both into their existing rationales.
