The ddev-migrate fixtures carried real-tree path literals from the
suite's birth (`/var/www/vhosts/...`, `/srv/other/outside`). The
first sandboxing wave left the literals in the fixtures and rewrote
them at runtime with a single `sed` line over the generated config —
inertness hung on that one rewrite surviving. The same-day maintainer
directive dropped the sed and moved the fixtures to sanctioned paths
at the source, where no rewrite can rot.

Check: `test-ddev-migrate.sh` writes `/var/tmp/opencode-ddev-mig-roots/...`
directly and carries no path-rewriting sed; every Policy-INPUT
allowlist entry in the sandbox suite carries a reason.

Source: maintainer directive 2026-10-04 — history 5a27358 (0.0.42e C1
introduced the sed rewrite) then a36c172 (0.0.42f dropped it,
fixtures sandboxed + obfuscated at the source).
