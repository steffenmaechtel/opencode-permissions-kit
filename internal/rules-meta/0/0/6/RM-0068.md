Every chain recorded in internal/reviews/ converged within 2–7
snapshots. A longer tail is taste ping-pong, not defect removal —
round 3+ agent time mostly re-litigates accepted residuals (convergence
data). The five-round cap converts that observation into a hard
handoff: past it, the call belongs to the maintainer, not to the loop.

Check: the longest chain in internal/reviews/ stays within the
converged range; chains hitting the cap are maintainer-closed.
