Fix-induced regressions are severity-independent in the corpus: the
0.0.46b W1 MED was caused by LOW fixes, and round 0.0.39d's three MED
were all introduced by the fix wave itself. "Did the fixes break or
lie?" is therefore the right question for LOW fixes too — a full pass
after every fix wave would be waste, but the diff-scope review of the
fix diff is the cheap instrument that answers it.

Amended 2026-10-31 (#324, recalibration 4): the old wording ("a wave
review of the fix diff") collided with wave as a TIER — the pilot's
§4.2 escalation sentence self-contradicted (wave staffing vs 1-axis
micro) and micro ran every time, disclosed per round. The staffing is
now explicit: 1-axis micro; wave staffing only when a NEW MED/HIGH
escalates scope.

Check: every code-touching round in recent cases has a 1-axis micro
successor regardless of the round's finding severities; wave-staffed
successors name the MED/HIGH escalation that triggered them.
