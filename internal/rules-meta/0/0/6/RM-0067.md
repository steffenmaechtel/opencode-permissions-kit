Fix-induced regressions are severity-independent in the corpus: the
0.0.46b W1 MED was caused by LOW fixes, and round 0.0.39d's three MED
were all introduced by the fix wave itself. "Did the fixes break or
lie?" is therefore the right question for LOW fixes too — a full pass
after every fix wave would be waste, but the wave review of the fix
diff is the cheap instrument that answers it.

Check: every code-touching round in recent cases has a wave-review
successor regardless of the round's finding severities.
