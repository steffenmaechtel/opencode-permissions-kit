Real-tree literals inside unit suites are one broken rewrite away from
live: the migrate fixtures carried `/var/www/vhosts` paths for months
behind a `sed` rewrite that a wave then dropped (maintainer directive
2026-10-04: fixture data is sandboxed at the source). The ratchet
therefore fails any path-shaped literal starting with a real-tree
prefix — quoted or not, in fixture strings as much as in commands —
because execution context is exactly what changes silently.
Policy-INPUT values (parse-only fixtures, never executed as paths) are
allowlisted with reasons; e2e scripts stay out of scope because
container paths are their legitimate subject.

Check: the ratchet still flags real-tree-prefixed literals in unit
suites; every allowlist entry carries its reason.

Source: maintainer directive 2026-10-04.
