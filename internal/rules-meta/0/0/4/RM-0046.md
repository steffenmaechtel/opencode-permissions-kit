agent/learnings.md is the distillate of the reviews-meta process:
every L-rule carries its evidence inline (the class-statistics line
above each rule) and links the dated run folders under
internal/reviews-meta/ — the falsifiable ground lives inside the
repo, not in the world outside the artifact. A meta per learning
would paraphrase that inline evidence, which the quality bar defines
as a defect (a rationale that merely restates the rule, RM-0027).
learnings.md therefore stays out of the audit scope; its lifecycle is
owned by the meta-analysis runs (the "When a rule fails" section
defines the re-run-driven escalation). The obligation to run the
checklist is itself a rule and gets its meta via AGENTS.md ## Rules
once that block lands.
