Maintainer observation 2026-10-19: subagent runtimes frequently
exceed the minute budgets with nothing enforcing them — the numbers
are guidance, honored at best most of the time, and unmeasured. A
failed subagent's wall-time is invisible too: on opencode-go, slow
tokens/s timed a thinking subagent out after ~10 minutes and the
main agent saw only "Failed" — with no hint that ~38 minutes of work
had already gone in. Launch/return timestamps in the checkpoint file
make both visible at near-zero cost and run as passive evidence
collection (gathered quietly alongside every review, enforced by
nothing): once enough passes carry them,
budgets can be A/B'd against verified-finding yield — the
Budget-evolution note in review-calibration.md has carried that idea
since the 2026-10-01 revision.

Check: checkpoint side files carry launch/return lines with
timestamps per subagent; a calibration pass aggregates them.

Source: maintainer proposal 2026-10-19 (direct chat); internal/
records/review-calibration.md, "Budget evolution".
