The stable channel is a byte-identical fast-forward of master, cut by
`make release` at release time: whatever sits on master at that moment
ships verbatim to every `KIT_CHANNEL=stable` install. `scripts/
release.sh` gates the tag and the mirror, never the content — a direct
commit on master reaches users without a PR's CI (unit suite plus both
e2e suites) and without review coverage. The rule mirrors the branch
protection on `stable` itself: merges only, no direct commits. The
variant-c amendment (2026-10-28, #291) delegates pushes and PR
creation to the agent BEHIND the same protection: every push runs CI,
the PR is the review unit, and required approvals = 1 plus the
merge-stays-with-the-maintainer clause keep the closure decision
human — the delegation changes WHO opens the PR, not what protects
master.

Check: `git log master` shows PR merges and release commits only, never
direct work commits; agent-pushed branches carry green CI runs and
maintainer-approved merges.

Source: internal/process/release-handling.md (Option B, hotfix policy);
CONTRIBUTING.md workflow step 4.
