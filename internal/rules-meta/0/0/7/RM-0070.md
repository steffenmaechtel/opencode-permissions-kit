A sweep that happened but is not recorded cannot be audited: the next
review would have to re-derive whether siblings were checked, which
is exactly the incomplete-closure blindness 0.0.44a exposed. The
resolution row is the ledger the next round starts from (the
"resolution is the ledger" rule), so
the sweep's out-of-list findings belong there — what it found beyond
the listed lines is the sweep's proof of work.

Check: resolutions of swept findings name what the sweep found beyond
the listed lines (including "nothing further").

provenance (0.0.44a)".
