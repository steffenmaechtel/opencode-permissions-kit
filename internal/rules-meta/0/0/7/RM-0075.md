one in 0.0.39d) tells the next reader how much to trust unverified
claims. Deleting them would also be dishonest history — 0.0.42e
showed a retraction can be partially over-turned by an external
reviewer's counter-feedback (S3 → LOW carve-out), which is only
possible if the retraction is still on record.
