A pipefail regression once downgraded 21 e2e checks to SKIP and the
suite still reported green — only the skipped-counter betrayed it.
Root-running scripts are the kit's highest blast-radius surface, and
"later" in practice means after the regression ships: between
reviews nothing else exercises the root paths end-to-end.

Check: e2e suites run green with zero skips immediately after any
root-script behavior change (commit-time, not release-time).
