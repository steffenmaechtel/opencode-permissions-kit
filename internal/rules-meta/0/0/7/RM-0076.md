Every class left unautomated re-costs a full reviewer pass: the
corpus's pinned classes (log.sh coverage, run-step enforcement for
CI suites, charset gates, project-path policy tests) each became a
guard in its fixing wave, and the next review spent its budget on
what automation cannot see instead. Deferring the pin defers it to a
context that no longer has the finding loaded.

Check: finding classes the corpus marked pinnable carry a test/lint
guard; new classes get their pin in the same wave.
