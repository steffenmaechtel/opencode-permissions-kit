The system's own texts are not exempt from the rot it exists to
catch, and the rollout proved it: within its first five days the
ID-width decision changed (five digits to four) while the migration
was still cheap, the phase-2 pending-unit valve was retired once its
five occupants had received metas, and one review-loop rule was
retired as obsolete one day after creation — its convergence fact
survives in review-calibration.md. A founding rule that exempted
itself would leave the record's own prose unratified, the exact
blind spot the system removes everywhere else.
