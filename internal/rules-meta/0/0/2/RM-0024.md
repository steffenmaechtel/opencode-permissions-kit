A hundred-folder or hundred-file node is a scroll wall in an IDE
tree; the maintainer's comfort criterion is 10-20 entries per node.
With 4-digit IDs, one digit per level buys that everywhere: three
levels, at most ten entries per node, leaf folders of exactly ten
metas — no wall anywhere, and the only dead level is the root `0/`
until IDs reach 1000. Pure digit math: computable from the token,
immune to rules moving between files, zero bookkeeping. Naming
precedent: `internal/reviews-meta/`. Migrated
repeatedly while young because later consumers — index, audit,
bookmarks — would have pinned the paths.

Check: every meta sits in `N/N/N/` matching its first three ID
digits; no node exceeds 10 entries.

Source: maintainer decision 2026-10-16 (NNNN + `N/N/N` after the
digit-math and alternatives walkthrough).
