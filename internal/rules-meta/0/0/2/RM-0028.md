At full rollout (~100 metas) a uniform short interval is a monthly
mass chore that gets ignored — an ignored report loses credibility; a
uniform long interval misses volatility. Escalating exactly one
ladder step per clean revisit adapts each rule to its observed
behavior: proven-stable rules age out, volatile ones keep returning.
`2y` as the max keeps even the most proven rule on some radar.

Check: after a few cycles, intervals diverge by findings history, not
uniformly.
