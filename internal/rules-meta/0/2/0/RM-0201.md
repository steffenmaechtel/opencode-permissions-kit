The coverage-claim rule for the AUDIT side ("Parallel reviewer
agents": never verify a coverage claim via the mechanism that makes
the claim) has a writing-side
twin that was uncaptured. PR #724 round b A1 (2026-11-21): round a's
sweep axis closed with a reviewer's coverage self-report over the
remaining suites; the fix-wave commit message restated that line as
its own assertion ("swept clean"), and the round b re-review found two
bare sites of exactly the class the line claimed gone. A commit
message or PR body that restates a reviewer checkpoint's coverage line
inherits its unverifiability and turns a self-report into the record's
assertion; git history keeps the wrong claim even after the correction
lands in later comments.

Check: completeness claims in recent commit messages and PR bodies
cite the session's own derivation (the grep or sweep pattern named) or
carry the attributed form — a bare restatement of a reviewer's
coverage line is the gap.

Source: issue #725 item 2 (PR #724 round b A1).
