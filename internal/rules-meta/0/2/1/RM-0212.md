Recurrence and severity are different axes: a rare topic (data loss, a
security class) may never reach any session count and still be the
most valuable one in the intake, while a quorum would park it
indefinitely (issue #755, accepted trade-off latent value loss). The
reverse case exists too — a topic three sessions confirm can still be
one the maintainer declines. Both overrides are judgment calls the
loop cannot make itself; they were the maintainer's decision of
2026-11-22.

Check: A prio:high issue is workable without a confirming session; a
hand-applied promoted label or a closing comment is the record of
either override.
