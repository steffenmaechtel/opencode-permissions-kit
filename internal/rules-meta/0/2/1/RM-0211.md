The docs-loop pilot sorted reflection topics by maintainer judgment,
and the maintainer holds the least evidence for generality: a one-off
and a recurring pattern read the same from the triage chair, on detail
topics above all (pin-test shapes) where there is no domain view to
tell them apart (issue #755, pain 4). Sessions hold that evidence —
the same stumble in independent sessions is the only observable signal
of generality the process has. Three was the maintainer's starting
quorum (chat 2026-11-22), to be recalibrated against the recurrence
data after the first weeks. Comment counts cannot serve as that
signal: one session may comment repeatedly.

Check: Re-run the count over the open feedback issues after the first
weeks: the share of topics reaching three, and the time they take,
decide whether the quorum moves.

Source: issue #755 (recurrence-based intake; quorum decision in the
maintainer chat of 2026-11-22).
