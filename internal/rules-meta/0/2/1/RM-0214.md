The pilot trigger counted open issues (five or more) because every
filed issue was already a worked-out bundle with evidence. Under
recurrence-based intake the open set is the intake itself — topics are
expected to stay below quorum by design (issue #755) — so an open-count
trigger fires on volume instead of evidence and re-creates the
maintainer overload the redesign exists to remove. Five promoted
single-topic issues is the starting value, carried over from the
pilot's batch calibration.

Check: With many open issues below quorum the trigger stays silent; it
fires on promoted issues only.

Source: issue #755.
