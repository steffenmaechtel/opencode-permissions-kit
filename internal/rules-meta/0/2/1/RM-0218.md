AGENTS.md is loaded into every session before any work starts, so its
bytes are paid by every session; it grew from 17,956 to 26,930 bytes
between 2026-11-15 and 2026-11-21 (commits a91e7879 to 5993337c), half
its size in the docs-loop pilot's first week, while the boot context of
the repository's lead sessions rose from a median 12,100 prompt tokens
in ISO week 2026-W46 to 14,571 in 2026-W47 (issue #755). An unwritten cap is an
intention: the review learnings record that error classes collapsed
only after an enforced process step existed (agent/learnings.md, Why
these rules exist). 27 KiB is the rounded size at the decision, a
first stop chosen by the maintainer — the long-term target is an open
research question (issue #768).

Check: wc -c AGENTS.md stays at or below 27,648; a red suite means
compress or move, never raise the cap without a maintainer decision.

Source: issue #755; issue #768.
