pr-00211: one commit declared "loop closes in bookkeeping"; a later
commit retracted it after the rotation deviation made the closure
claim non-authoritative — history now carries both. Closure is a
stop-rule check over a round's dispositions, and the round that made
them is the interested party. The PR-native form makes the
confirmation structural: the closing summary may only PROPOSE
(the closing-proposed marker), and the maintainer's merge of the PR
is the confirmation — the bot cannot merge its own PR (approvals = 1),
so no loop can close itself even by accident.

Expected effect: closure claims appear as proposals with the cited
check; the merged PR is the only closed state.

Check: closing summaries carry the closing-proposed marker with the
stop-rule check cited; no loop-written comment or commit declares
the loop closed.

Source: issue #214 item 10 (pr-00211, rounds c–e); the pilot
retrospectives on #278.
