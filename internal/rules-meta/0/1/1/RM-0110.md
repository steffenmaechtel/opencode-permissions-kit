session — a rotation deviation. The recovery (status stays open,

deviation that can still happen: a pass that ran main-solo, or on

verdicts stay non-authoritative; the successor's two-part mandate

instead of an improvised one; successors find their two-part mandate

timeline.

still open plus a comment naming the deviation and the successor
mandate.
