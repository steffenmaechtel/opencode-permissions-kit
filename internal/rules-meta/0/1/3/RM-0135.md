A meta's approval certifies specific content; once that content
changes, the certification is stale. Until 2026-10-31 the system had
no expression for that state: two behavior-bearing metas amended in
the review-track codification kept their old approval date in the
frontmatter (invariant 9 requires the pairing), so the generated
INDEX rendered both as approved and the Review-pending worklist
omitted them — their own logs said "maintainer review pending" while
every queue showed nothing to do.

Check: the INDEX Actionable section lists every approved-then-amended
meta under "Amended — pending"; the audit blocks the fieldless shape
(invariant 9).

Source: issue #333 (finding A2 from the #332 round-a review).
