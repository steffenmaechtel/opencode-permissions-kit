A meta's approval certifies specific content; once that content
changes, the certification is stale. Until 2026-10-31 the system had
no expression for that state: two behavior-bearing metas amended in
the review-track codification kept their old approval date in the
frontmatter (invariant 9 requires the pairing), so the generated
INDEX rendered both as approved and the Review-pending worklist
omitted them — their own logs said "maintainer review pending" while
every queue showed nothing to do. The letter later stood against the
sanctioned practice it governs: the 2026-11-20 blank-line sweep
(pr-00694 round a B1) edited fifteen approved metas under a
maintainer directive with no amendment trail, and no written
carve-out existed — a divergence invisible to CI by construction
(invariant 9 keys on amendment lines only). The whitespace-only
exception is therefore part of the rule: layout normalization under
a maintainer directive does not re-queue. The freshness half was
enforced by the audit before it was authorable: PR #742's birth
commit (2026-11-22) left the previous amendment's date in the
frontmatter over a newer amendment log line and reddened invariant
9's stale class — one gate cycle paid for the coupling the rule text
now states (issue #744).

Check: the INDEX Actionable section lists every approved-then-amended
meta under "Amended — pending"; the audit blocks the fieldless shape
(invariant 9).

Source: issue #333 (finding A2 from the #332 round-a review).
