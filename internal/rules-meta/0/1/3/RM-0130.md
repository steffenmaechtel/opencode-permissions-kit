Born from maintainer guidance (2026-10-30, the #314 follow-up session):
review and fix waves were repeatedly observed spending hours
fine-polishing pins that already worked — fixture elegance and wording
churn — while the shipped code, the surface that runs on user machines,
got the smaller share of the attention. The directive: pins must work
well — discriminate, sit before the summary gate (the learnings L4
floor) — they do not have to be perfect; the review process itself
(rounds re-reviewing every fix wave, sabotage spot-checks, the standing
suites) catches pin drift when a real defect shows the need, so
stopping at the floor is safe.

Check: pin-style findings past the floor surface in round comments as
deliberately-not-done (taste, scope brake channel) or deferred issues,
not as fix waves; review time goes to files/ and its templates first.
