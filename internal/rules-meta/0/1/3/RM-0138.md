Two loop sessions in consecutive cases (#407 and #412, both 2026-11)
dropped the loop at the same position: the re-review of a fix wave
was not treated as a round. #407 posted rounds b–e as one batch after
their fixes; #412 fixed round b's findings (B2 in cb4b2384) and
posted round b's comment only on the maintainer's request — the
comment's dispositions table already cited the fix SHAs. The
roundness of a re-review was derivable (the lettering rule's shared
letter, the tier ladder's "EVERY fix-wave re-review" row) but stated
nowhere, and the wave-letter vocabulary of handovers ("fix wave b"
for round a's fixes) offered a reading in which the re-review is a
minor comment-less check.

Check: on every birth-PR timeline each round — re-reviews included —
carries its own round comment whose posted timestamp predates its
round's first fix commit, and no fix push is the last event (a round
comment or a closing summary follows).

Source: maintainer directive 2026-11-07 (PR #412).
