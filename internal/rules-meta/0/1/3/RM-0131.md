Pilot batch 1 (issue #324, six non-shipped PRs, 2026-10-31): with
1–2-axis staffing the batch ran 2h17m wall clock — versus ~4h for
five PRs the day before — while catching 6 MED/HIGH, including a
wave-introduced HIGH and 9+ class-sweep siblings the implementation
sweeps had missed. The constraint was implementation sweep quality,
not reviewer depth: a single flash axis did not under-review. Review
cost scales with round count and staffing, not diff size — a 31-line
and a 456-line diff cost nearly the same at equal rounds.

Check: MED markers carry 1–2 reviewers; escalations to wave staffing
name the MED/HIGH finding that triggered them.
