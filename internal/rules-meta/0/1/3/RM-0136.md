work orders were recorded in round comments (and the PR body) with no
step that ever turns them into tracker issues. PR #338's closing
summary carried the PROJECT_TILDE_HOME duplication exactly that way,
and follow-up issue #340 exists only because the maintainer asked for
it in chat — unprompted, the agent's process had no duplicate-check
against the tracker, no defined moment for the ask, and no bundling
step for multi-topic loops; a maintainer who reads only the end of a
report (the documented reading habit behind the round-comment status
line) never saw the offer at all.

Check: notification mails still strip details content (the documented
cause of the end-only reading habit), and post-2026-11 loops file their
work-order issues through the gate — a chat-rescued offer would be the
ungated fact returning.
