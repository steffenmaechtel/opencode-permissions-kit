The work-order gate's fold arm keys on the matched issue's scope, but
nothing made that scope legible: PR #657's gate folded two
shipped-code findings into #573, and whether #573 was the right home
(file class) was answerable only by re-deriving the ledger's
composition from its full body — the title said "recorded LOW/INFO
findings (not fixed in wave a)", the policy parenthetical said
"non-shipped-track" while the ledger's items were overwhelmingly
shipped-code files/** findings. The main session had to do the body
archaeology; the maintainer had to ask which class the ledger even
collected. A collection issue that states its own composition in the
title or preamble makes the same decision a one-glance read.
Check: a deferred-findings collection issue's title or first lines
name its file class, severity range and source pass without opening
the body.
Source: issue #658 (PR #657 work-order gate).
