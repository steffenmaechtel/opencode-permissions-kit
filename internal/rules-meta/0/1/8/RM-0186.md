The review loop leans on fan-out subagents with checkpoint files, and
sessions die in the wild: PR #655's round b security reviewer was
mid-review when the maintainer accidentally closed the opencode
window — the completion notification never arrived, the checkpoint
file carried only its launch line, and the first instinct (re-spawn a
fresh subagent with the full prompt) would have thrown away the
partial work, the finding-ID namespace and the provenance narrative.
A follow-up subagent call WITH the dead child's sessionID joins the
existing session and continues it; the restarted reviewer delivered
its full four sections, and the round b comment discloses the restart
in its provenance line.
Check: a fan-out whose session died mid-run is recovered by a
sessionID-joining follow-up call, not a fresh re-spawn, and the
restart is disclosed in run provenance.
Source: issue #667 (PR #655 round b).
