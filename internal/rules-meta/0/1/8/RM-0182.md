A wave that codifies a discipline handles the rule text and its own
new prose in one pass, and the rule's freshness works against its
author: pr-00629 round a found three violations of the wave's own
fresh rules inside the same diff — a pointer coined for the occasion
that greps nowhere (written into the citation rule's own item), a
name colliding with a heading in the file being edited, and a count
its cited source contradicts two sentences later. All three were
caught by the fresh-context review pass, none by the wave's gate
(issue #632). The class recurred on the rule's own landing day:
pr-00634 round a A1 found the same wave's amended check-3 bullet in
conventions.md carrying two opposite scope models unreconciled —
the fresh sweep's enumeration covered names, pointers and counts,
not phrase-level consistency with the section's sibling text, so it
could not catch its own wave's sibling edit (issue #636). A third
evidence shape arrived the same day: pr-00640 round a Q1 found an
incident compression drifted from a record the wave had itself
verified — the pr-00634 A1 carrier named as the sweep's own fresh
bullet instead of the wave's amended check-3 bullet (issue #641).
The rule's own second wave supplied the read-depth and mirror
shapes: pr-00643 round a Q2 found the sweep's sibling-read applied
at phrase depth (the clause's origin line) over a bullet whose
adjacent unit's other legs reach outside the section — the pair gave
opposite answers about the sweep's reach — and round a Q1 found the
wave's enumeration edit had extended the rule bullet but left the
meta's Check line one leg behind: the Check mirrors the bullet's
enumeration, and a grep cannot find an absence (issue #645).
Check: a doc wave's diff greps its own new pointer names in their
targets, collision-checks its new names against the target file's
headings and leads, reconciles its counts, re-reads its incident
compressions against the records it consulted, reads its new clauses
against the sibling text of their whole adjacent units (every leg,
not the clause's origin line), and reconciles an enumerated rule
unit's mirror texts (the meta's Check line) against the full new
enumeration before the gate.
Source: issues #632, #636, #641 and #645.
