A capability claim over a set duplicates the current result of a
query over a growing set even when it names no members: docs/
reference/files.md claimed `#` lines are "ignored by every reader"
(status counts, the doctor scan roots, the config/update refresh
passes) while the wrapper's refusal screen — a reader the wave never
touched — printed them and counted them toward its 10-row cap (PR
#670 round a, findings A2 + C1, curated L1). The wave had verified
the readers it changed; the sentence generalized that subset. The
fix made the claim true reader-side, but the next reader added to
the tree would falsify the sentence again unless the claim carries
its scope or points at the query.

Check: grep the whole reader/consumer set for the claimed capability
before the sentence ships, and re-grep at review; a sentence whose
set is not greppable in one pattern is scoped to the verified subset
instead.

Source: issue #673 (PR #670 round a, findings A2 and C1, curated
as L1).
