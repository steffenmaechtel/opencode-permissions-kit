The comments-only in-round exception is easy to over-read: it
waives the FOLLOW-ON round (no re-review owed after a docs-only
fix), and under time pressure that reads as license to fix before
the round comment exists. The pr-00605 loop made exactly that miss:
round b's single INFO finding was fixed and committed (931a8bd1)
before the Review b comment posted — a violation of the
round-comment-before-fix hard seam, disclosed in that round's run
provenance with the prescribed recovery. The finding must be inspectable on the timeline while
nothing has been done about it, whatever the change kind; the
mechanical posted.log check holds for docs-only fixes too.

Check: the newest case's round comments each precede their round's
first fix commit in the timeline order.

Source: issue #607.
