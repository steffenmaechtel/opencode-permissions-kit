The fail-loud contract breaks at the call site as quietly as in the
producer: an `|| true` consumer converts producer death into a clean
verdict. The corpus's most expensive instance is the mktemp-fallback
chain (`0.0.48b W2` root `rm -f /dev/null` → `pr-00221 a W3`
false-green over a dead scan). The sweep waves' counter-shape is
uniform in the tree: ten `if ! producer … >"$out"; then` call sites
pass the producer's rc to the suite (test-docs.sh:161,
test-rules-meta.sh:717/791, test-sandbox-policy.sh:255/283/372/375/
391/495/522), the remaining call sites are the executed death arms
themselves (`|| rc=$?` capture) or `|| exit 1` fail-loud legs — zero
`|| true` consumers anywhere.

Check: grep detector call sites for `|| true`; every consumer must be
able to fail the suite.
