PR #710's rebuilt probe lost its diagnostics twice in review before
this shape settled: the diagnostic rerun carried `2>&1`, discarding
the very stderr it existed to show (round b D1), and the first draft
had no plain passthrough to degrade to at all (round a B1). Two
degrade spellings are both sanctioned and shipped — the tee-side
`cat` passthrough with one warn line naming the lost counter
(fs-baseline, git-heal-core), and the consumer-side skip that never
starts the tee (status-core, silent by design since #244 c S3: the
probe's own unswallowed stderr is the signal). In both spellings the
stream keeps its data and loses only the live counter.

Check: the broken-interpreter arm of each pin suite asserts data
integrity in the site's own spelling — passthrough rows still arrive,
or the consumer-side skip still produces the scan list — and the
loss is named (warn line, or the probe's stderr visible).

Source: issue #714 incident 1 (B1, D1); status-core.sh:165–185 (the
silent-skip design).
