A `command -v python3` check accepts a findable-but-broken
interpreter: a pyenv shim pointing at a removed version answers
`command -v` with a path and then exits nonzero with its diagnostic
on stderr (probed 2026-11-21 with a shim on PATH). A tee started
with such an interpreter dies at pipeline startup and hands the
consuming loop an empty scan list with no red rc at the consumer —
the silent false-green #221 a W3 recorded for status-core's scan.
The probe runs `python3 -I -c '<the tee's imports>'` because that is
the exact execution being gated.

Check: every shipped python3 tee gates on a same-invocation-shape
health probe (not `command -v`), and the pin suites execute the
broken-interpreter arm (the #244 c S3 shape, test-status.sh and
test-git-heal-core.sh).

Source: issue #714 incident 1; PR #710 round a B1 (all three axes
independently).
