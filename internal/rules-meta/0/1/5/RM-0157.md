docs-loop.md's first draft of the from-reflection corpus sentence
enumerated the extract refiles as "(class extract, #541–#543)" —
reading as the corpus roster; the extract class exists to create
more refiles, so the fourth one would silently falsify the standing
prose (PR #555 round a, finding a Q2; fixed in 00696229 as "the
first three were #541–#543"). The same trap is an unscoped count of
a growing set ("the whole population of 14") — falsified by the
next member, not seconds later but just as silently.

Check: grep standing prose for exhaustive enumerations of growing
sets and unscoped counts — each scoped to its observation moment or
reworded as history.
