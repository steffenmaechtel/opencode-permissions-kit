Verification earned its cost in every pilot: P2 — 2 of 3 refutations
from verifiers, zero main-audit overrides; P3 — all 3 refutations,
including the load-bearing b C7 that invalidated the round-a MED
fix; Case A — 1 refutation (Q6, the reviewer had missed a second
operand). Re-read 2026-11-07 (maintainer, with revision 2 below):
their value was never fresh eyes — fresh context is load-bearing at
the REVIEW pass, not at verification; verify subagents exist for
main-session context economy at volume and parallel re-derivation
across 2–3 sessions, and the pilot refutations above came from
exactly those volume-zone batches.

Amended 2026-10-31 (#324, decisions 3 + recalibration 3): one
verifier batch was skipped under time pressure (#329 round b) and
only the disclosed deviation caught it — the sizing is a hard gate
now. The ≤ 3 LOW/INFO inline case generalizes the micro-row
exception (0 verifier launches at 0–2 findings was already standing
practice in micro rounds).

Revised 2026-11-02 (maintainer decision at approval): the inline
case rises from ≤ 3 to ≤ 5 curated findings (all LOW/INFO) — small
sets verify in the main session without a verifier batch. The
2026-10-31 threshold above is era evidence; rule text and the
skill's two sizing sites swept in the same change.

Revised 2026-11-07 (#409, staffing-economics run
[reviews-meta 2026-11-07](../../../../reviews-meta/2026-11-07/)): the
count arm is dropped — sizing is severity-driven now. A LOW/INFO-only
round verifies main-inline at ANY finding count; any MED/HIGH
finding still owes exactly one batch. The numbers: all six
LOW/INFO-only rounds with > 5 curated findings (gate era) ran a
strong batch for at most one unattributable retraction (~34 min,
0.33M in-tok), while main-inline rounds retract LOW/INFO ghosts too
and the next round's fresh re-review nets the rest. The severity
arm stays at ANY MED/HIGH and not at ≥ 3 MED/HIGH findings (the
alternative considered): its yield spreads across both count bands —
6 of 8 retraction rounds carried only 1–2 MED/HIGH (gate era: 29
rounds in the 1–2 band → 7 retractions, 8 rounds ≥ 3 → 2). The
2026-11-02 ≤ 5 threshold above is era evidence; rule text and the
skill's two sizing sites swept in the same change.

Revised again 2026-11-07 (maintainer chat review of the severity
form, same day): sizing is COUNT-driven — ≤ 10 curated findings
verify main-inline (main fully re-derives every MED/HIGH, mandatory
in every shape); > 10 owes one verifier batch of 2–3 parallel
strong sessions. Rationale: the finding generator is a fresh
reviewer either way; main holds the digest after curation and
verifies small sets faster than a subagent bootstraps; past ~10
inline checks main's context grows too far in late waves. Yield by
size (gate era): retraction RATE is ~30% of rounds above 10 curated
(4 of 13: #344 a, #357 a, #360 a, #370 a) vs ~12% below (#367 a,
#339 a, #309 b — 3 of ~24) — the direction supports the threshold,
but sub-10 yield is NOT zero, and the sub-10 retractions happened
WITH batches: whether main-inline catches them is unmeasured (no
counterfactual) — a design bet on the three nets (fresh reviewer,
mandatory re-derivation, next round's fresh re-review), to be
re-checked at the 2026-11-28 revisit. #367 a (3 findings incl. the
round's HIGH) stands as the sharpest counter-datapoint. The severity-only form above lasted one day; rule text
and the skill's two sizing sites swept again.

Check: verdict files exist per owed round; the comment's findings
