Round comments carry full finding mechanisms for MED/HIGH only (the
round comment contract nests LOW/INFO as one line plus anchor), and
session scratch under /tmp dies with its host — so an issue filed
with a one-liner loses its evidence the moment the filing session
ends. The first from-review filing batch (#569–#573) had to be
rescued by retroactive evidence-appendix comments mined from verifier
files; #573 alone re-attached 34 findings.
Check: pick a filed from-review work order and confirm its body
carries file:line and mechanism inline — no pointer to scratch or to
round-comment detail blocks.
