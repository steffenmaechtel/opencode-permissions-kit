A docker-exec'd e2e container has no controlling terminal: opening
/dev/tty fails immediately (it does not block) — which is precisely
the scenario a /dev/tty guard exists for. The old run.sh 12d comment
claimed blocking and was wrong (pr-00568 wave a); display tests for
interactive menus need a real pty, and no-tty contract tests pin the
loud fast abort with a timeout as the no-hang discriminator.

Check: the e2e menu-display arm runs under a pty harness and the
no-tty arm asserts rc 1 plus the guard message inside a timeout.

Source: issue #575 §4; tests/e2e/run.sh.
