During the pr-00211 release review, the reviewing agent twice started
the wave review (snapshot b) after the FIRST single-finding fix
instead of after the round's complete dispositions: "fix F1 first,
report when done" was read as "the wave = F1". The premature snapshot
is committed and immutable — the chain had to continue with the next
free letter, diluting the diff each review covers.

Expected effect: wave reviews keep covering complete rounds; ad-hoc
sequencing instructions stop splitting one round into several
half-reviewed waves.

Check: a round's wave-review snapshot covers the full fix diff of all
its findings, not a single-finding subset; mid-round sequencing
instructions are quoted in the resolution.

Source: issue #213 item 1 (pr-00211 post-mortem).
