Ten simultaneous flash-tier reviewer launches in the pr-00211 release
review hit the provider's account-wide burst limit; three restarts
were needed to drain the queue. A concurrency cap is meaningless
without a staging rule — the burst limit counts launches per time
window, not per model.

Expected effect: fan-outs stay inside provider burst limits on every
tier; no restart loops mid-review.

Check: review runs stage launches (5 seats, backfill on return)
instead of one n-at-once batch; run provenance notes the staging.

Source: issue #213 item 5 (pr-00211 post-mortem).
