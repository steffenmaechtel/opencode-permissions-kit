The rotation-era intro ("Inside a review session, the reviewer
mechanics below apply unchanged") was read in pr-00211 as permitting
in-session review passes to satisfy the rotation. The PR-native
rewrite resolves the question at the root: freshness is a property
of the REVIEWING CONTEXT, not of the session boundary — every
reviewer and verifier subagent is fresh by construction, and
Session MAIN reviewing its own wave solo is banned at every tier
(low tier included). The single-session case is the normal shape now,
not a self-declared exception needing sanction.

Expected effect: freshness questions resolve to subagent contexts;
no main-solo pass at any tier; single-session cases need no
per-case USER sanction.

Check: round comments' metadata names subagent reviewers/verifiers
for every pass; no loop status records a main-solo review.

Source: issue #213 item 6 (pr-00211 post-mortem); the decision
re-based on subagent contexts by the PR-native rewrite (#291, pilot
retrospectives on #278).
