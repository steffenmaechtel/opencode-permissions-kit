Computing the next date from the stale due date compounds lateness: a
rule reviewed two months late would be instantly overdue again, and
the overdue report fills with entries no work can ever clear. The
revisit work happened on the revisit date — that is the only honest
anchor.

Check: after an overdue revisit, `next-revisit` is revisit date +
interval, strictly in the future.

Source: maintainer decision 2026-10-14 (interval policy).
