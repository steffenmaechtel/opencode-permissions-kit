Developers search their IDE — often plain text, no regex — for a
token like `RM-00029.md`; that search must return the meta and its
genuinely referencing sites, not 40 decorative hits. A citation that
looks structural but is decoration sends a reader — or the future
audit — chasing a "reference" that carries no weight. Refs belong in
exactly two places: the rule unit's trailing reference, and genuine
dependencies inside a rationale body. Review logs narrate without
numbers.

Check: an IDE text search for a meta's token returns the meta, its
rule unit's ref, and at most load-bearing dependency mentions.
