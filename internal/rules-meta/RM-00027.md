The system's whole detection mechanism is "test whether the
rationale's fact still holds". A paraphrase of the rule offers
nothing to test, so the rule looks identical whether current or
obsolete — the pre-system status quo, just with paperwork. Only
falsifiable claims turn a revisit into a real check.

Check: every rationale names at least one external fact checkable
against code, versions, or the environment.
