Unit suites run on real contributor hosts, without containers. An
empty variable turns `"$VAR/sub"` into `/sub` — a real delete target.
A pure variable (`rm -f "$CONF"`) is a harmless no-op instead. That
is the whole reason for the `:?` guard on every var+suffix spelling.

Check: `test-sandbox-policy.sh` still enforces it; no new unguarded
var+suffix `rm` in `tests/` or `files/`.
